I've cut another release of the stern brocot terminal application!
https://github.com/montokapro/stern-brocot/releases#release-v2.0.0

This version adds many more explanatory modes, a more straightforward interface, and several more ways of multiplying matrices. We can navigate Calkin-Wilf, its inverse, and multiply using two other matrices (plus their inverses).
